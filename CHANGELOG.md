# Changelog

## 0.1.0.dev0

- Joint Gaussian shrinkage with canonical, supplied and learned covariance patterns.
- EE/EZ scales, correlated errors and exact observed-block missing-condition inference.
- Posterior distributions, contrasts, sharing summaries and batched prior reuse.
- JSON persistence, labeled tables, plotting and numerical reference checks.
- Native multivariate Student-t summary errors with checked numerical quadrature.
- Direct Gaussian, binomial, Poisson and NB2 regression with Gaussian-mixture coefficient priors.
- Cox and Weibull survival, multinomial and ordinal regression, and Poisson/NB zero-inflated and hurdle models.
- Gaussian group intercepts and slopes; posterior sampling diagnostics, prediction, result persistence and regression plots.
- Independent distribution and finite-case posterior checks for the added methods.
